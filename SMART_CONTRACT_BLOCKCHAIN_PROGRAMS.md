# Smart Contract & Blockchain Bug Bounty Programs

## HackerOne and Bugcrowd — No Deposit/Fee Required to Submit

Both HackerOne and Bugcrowd are **completely free for researchers**. You sign up, find a program, and submit reports at no cost. The company (not the researcher) pays the platform. This is unlike Immunefi, which charges a 10% performance fee on top of the researcher's reward and requires KYC for payouts.

## Key Notes for Researchers

1. **HackerOne and Bugcrowd charge nothing to researchers** — sign up, submit, get paid.
2. **Private programs** (marked above) require an invitation. Build reputation on public programs first.
3. **Smart contract scope varies** — some programs (e.g., Coinbase) split smart contract bugs to a separate platform (Cantina) while keeping offchain bugs on HackerOne.
4. **Verify scope before testing** — always read the program policy page for in-scope assets and rules of engagement.
5. **Bounty amounts change** — check the program page for current reward tiers before investing time.
